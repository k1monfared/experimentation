Chapter 3 sandbox: power, N, and MDE

Three knobs: the true alternative bias p_alt, the per-arm sample size N, and alpha. Power is the probability of rejecting the null at H0: p = 0.5 when the true bias is p_alt. Watch how the curves move when you change the knobs. The MDE is the smallest |p_alt - 0.5| at which the chosen N reaches your power target.

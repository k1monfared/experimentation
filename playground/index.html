Playground

Single-page widgets that let you play with the central knob of each chapter. Each one re-implements just the bit of math it needs in JavaScript so it runs in your browser with no setup.

Chapter 1 - The coin (sandbox)

One slider on the true bias p. Toss one at a time, in tens, in hundreds. Watch the running fraction settle and the Bayesian posterior tighten on the same picture.

open →

Chapter 2 - The p-value, visualized

Pick N and observed k. Watch the binomial PMF under H0, the rejection region for one- vs two-sided alternatives, and the running p-value as the area of the tails. The picture every p-value ultimately points back to.

open →

Chapter 3 - Power, sample size, MDE

Drag the true alternative p_alt, the per-arm N, and the alpha. The widget shows the power-vs-N curve, the power-vs-p_alt curve, and reads off the required-N and the MDE at your chosen power target.

open →

Chapter 5 - Confidence interval sweeper

Same data, four 95% intervals: Wald, Wilson, Clopper-Pearson, Bayesian (Beta 1,1). Slide k/N to the boundary and watch Wald collapse while the others stay sensible. The width-vs-N panel shows how each one shrinks.

open →

Chapter 6 - Prior tuner

Drag a Beta(a, b) prior. Set the observed k heads in N tosses. Watch the posterior re-render. Crank N up and see the prior get drowned out.

open →

Chapter 12 - Simpson's paradox sandbox

Two segments. Drag the segment sizes and the per-segment treatment shares. Watch the aggregate sign flip while every segment stays positive.

open →

Chapter 14 - Novelty triple-view

Run a small synthetic experiment with novelty-decay treatment. Then look at the same data three ways: calendar day, days-since-first-exposure, and per-user exposure count. Three different X axes, three different shapes of decay, one underlying data set.

open →

Chapter 18 - Shipping cost simulator

Loads 2,000 simulated A/B tests and lets you drag the cost ratio (FP cost vs miss cost). Watch which lens has lower expected cost per decision under the chosen risk profile.

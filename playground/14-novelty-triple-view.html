Chapter 14 sandbox: same data, three measurement schemes

Run a small synthetic experiment with a novelty-decay treatment effect. The treatment lift starts at initial_lift on the day of first exposure and decays with half-life half_life_days. The control rate is flat. Then look at the same data three ways: calendar day (days from launch), days since the user's first exposure, and exposure count for that user. Three different X axes, three different shapes of decay, one underlying data set.
